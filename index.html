    Alignment check

    Overlay the outline of another map's data footprint on top of the current one. If the georeferencing is right, coastlines and state edges should coincide.

    Cells off land is the decisive test: it needs no second study, so it separates georeferencing from scope. A map is registered if its best shift is near (0°, 0°) and the gain over 0% is small. Coarse grids legitimately place coastal cell centres offshore, so judge the shift, not the raw percentage. Mask overlap and best shift compare two studies and are confounded by the different areas each one chose to simulate — read them second.
